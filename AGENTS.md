# website-ecrow.io Application

Marketing and informational website for ecrow.io built with Next.js and React.

## Business Description

eCrow Ltd. develops and operates web-based software products, including software-as-a-service (SaaS) applications and browser-based digital games.

The company sells access to its software through subscriptions, one-time purchases, and in-app digital services. Individual products may be marketed under distinct brand names, all of which are owned and operated by eCrow Ltd.
